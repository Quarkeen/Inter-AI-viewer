# IIIT Dharwad — Online M.Tech DSAI Admissions

**Proposed admissions-readiness profile for faculty review.** This preserves the description drafted for the project; it is not an official interview syllabus published by IIIT Dharwad.

Programme reference: [IIIT Dharwad Online M.Tech in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence](https://onlinedsai.iiitdwd.ac.in/)

## Programme overview

The online M.Tech in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at IIIT Dharwad combines academic study, practical work and research. This admissions interview assesses foundational knowledge, problem-solving ability and readiness for postgraduate learning.

## Applicant profile

Applicants should meet the institute’s published qualifying-degree and academic eligibility requirements, subject to verification by the admissions team.

Fresh graduates and working professionals are welcome. Relevant preparation may come from coursework, independent study, academic projects, internships, research or professional experience.

## Foundations to assess

### Programming and problem solving

Understand variables, conditions, loops, functions and basic data structures. Explain a simple algorithm and test it against examples and edge cases. Python familiarity is useful; another programming language or clear pseudocode is acceptable.

### Mathematical reasoning

Demonstrate basic understanding of vectors, matrices, functions, derivatives, probability and descriptive statistics. Explain the reasoning behind an answer rather than only recalling formulas.

### Data reasoning

Recognise missing values, outliers and limitations in a dataset. Choose appropriate summaries and question unsupported conclusions.

### Learning and research readiness

Explain a project, assignment or problem personally investigated. Describe the approach, individual contribution, validation, limitations and lessons learned.

### Motivation and responsibility

Explain the interest in studying DSAI and demonstrate awareness of honest reporting, responsible data use and academic integrity.

## Helpful exposure

Introductory machine learning, Python data tools, SQL, scientific computing or relevant projects can support the discussion.

## Admissions assessment principles

Assess reasoning and demonstrated understanding at entry level. Accept valid alternative approaches and equivalent explanations. Professional employment, publications, paid certifications and advanced AI-framework expertise are not requirements of this proposed interview profile.
